GhostTab Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026 · Published by Moonwick Software

GhostTab is a Chrome extension that closes browser tabs after a period of inactivity you choose. It is built to work without collecting information about you or the pages you visit.

What GhostTab does not collect

  • GhostTab does not read the content, address (URL) or title of any page you visit, and does not request permission to do so.
  • GhostTab does not collect browsing history, analytics or usage statistics.
  • GhostTab has no server of its own and sends no data anywhere, except the Premium check described below.

What is stored on your device

  • Your settings (timeout, which tab types to close, whether the counter is shown) are saved with Chrome's storage. If Chrome sync is on, Chrome syncs them to your own Google account. GhostTab cannot access that account.
  • The last time each tab was used is kept in Chrome's session storage, only while Chrome is open, and is deleted when Chrome closes. It contains internal tab numbers and times only, never page addresses or titles.
  • Whether Premium is unlocked is cached on your device.

Premium purchases

Premium is an optional one-time purchase handled by ExtensionPay, with payments processed by Stripe. When you buy Premium or restore a purchase, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe processes your payment details. GhostTab never sees or stores your card details. To check whether Premium is unlocked, GhostTab asks ExtensionPay for your purchase status when you open the popup.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your data. The only third parties involved are ExtensionPay and Stripe, and only when you choose to buy Premium.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling GhostTab removes its settings and stored data from your browser.
  • To ask about or delete purchase records, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
