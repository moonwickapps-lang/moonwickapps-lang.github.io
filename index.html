GhostTab for Chrome

Tabs you forget close themselves.

GhostTab is a Chrome extension that closes tabs you stopped using after a time you choose, including Incognito tabs, so a forgotten private window never stays open on a shared computer.

Launching on the Chrome Web Store, November 2026

What GhostTab does

Closes idle tabs

Pick a timeout from 5 minutes to 8 hours. Tabs you haven't touched in that time close on their own.

Private by design

Incognito tabs can close too. GhostTab never reads your pages or browsing history and sends nothing to a server.

Never in your way

The tab you're on, pinned tabs, tabs playing audio and your last tab are never closed.

Closing-soon counter

A small number on the icon shows how many tabs will close in the next minute.

GhostLock for Chrome

Lock any tab. Or make it look unlocked.

GhostLock hides a tab behind a PIN in one click. With Stealth lock, the tab turns into an ordinary page, so nobody can tell anything is hidden. Your PIN is never stored.

Coming to the Chrome Web Store, January 2027

What GhostLock does

One-click lock

Lock the tab you're on from the toolbar, a right-click or Alt+Shift+L. One PIN opens every locked tab.

Stealth lock

The locked tab becomes Google or a site you choose. No lock screen, no Back button back to the page.

Your PIN stays yours

GhostLock never stores your PIN, and locked addresses are encrypted on your computer. No account, no server.

Incognito that locks itself

Private tabs can lock after a few minutes without use, or the moment your screen locks.

Decoy PIN

Type your PIN backwards and the tab opens a harmless site instead, while the real page stays hidden.

Touch ID

Unlock with your fingerprint on a Mac. Your PIN always works too.

Auto-lock and auto-close

Lock or close tabs you stopped using, and always lock chosen sites like email or banking when you switch away.

Lock all, unlock all

Hide every tab at once before you hand over your laptop or share your screen.
