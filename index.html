GhostTab for Chrome

Tabs you forget close themselves.

GhostTab is a Chrome extension that closes tabs you stopped using after a time you choose, including Incognito tabs, so a forgotten private window never stays open on a shared computer.

Launching on the Chrome Web Store, November 2026

What GhostTab does

Closes idle tabs

Pick a timeout from 5 minutes to 8 hours. Tabs you haven't touched in that time close on their own.

Private by design

Incognito tabs can close too. GhostTab never reads your pages or browsing history and sends nothing to a server.

Never in your way

The tab you're on, pinned tabs, tabs playing audio and your last tab are never closed.

Closing-soon counter

A small number on the icon shows how many tabs will close in the next minute.
