Using our extensions

Moonwick Software extensions, including GhostTab and GhostLock, are free to install from the Chrome Web Store. By installing one, you agree to use it in line with the Chrome Web Store terms and applicable law. Extensions are provided "as is", without warranty of any kind.

GhostTab Premium

GhostTab Premium is an optional one-time purchase that unlocks extra features in the extension. It is not a subscription and is never charged again. Premium is tied to the email you use at checkout and can be restored on other browsers with that email.

GhostLock Pro

GhostLock Pro is an optional subscription, billed monthly ($1) or yearly ($9) until you cancel. A 14-day free trial is available inside the extension without a card. You can cancel anytime; Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for and is not charged again. Pro is tied to the email you use at checkout and can be restored on other browsers with that email.

GhostLock is a privacy screen for your tabs. It keeps casual onlookers out, but it is not a replacement for locking your computer and does not protect against someone who can change your browser's settings. If you forget your PIN, it cannot be recovered; resetting GhostLock closes your locked tabs. Unlocking your tabs never requires Pro.
