GhostLock Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026 · Published by Moonwick Software

GhostLock is a Chrome extension that locks browser tabs behind a PIN. Everything it does happens on your computer. It has no server of its own and does not collect information about you.

What GhostLock reads

  • When you lock a tab, GhostLock reads that tab's address (URL) so it can reopen the page when you unlock it. This is why Chrome shows the "Read your browsing history" permission.
  • GhostLock does not read page content, does not collect browsing history, and has no analytics or usage tracking.

What is stored on your device

  • Your PIN is never stored. GhostLock keeps only a scrambled check value (PBKDF2 with a random salt) and an encryption key that your PIN protects.
  • Locked tab addresses are saved encrypted in Chrome's local storage, so only your PIN (or Touch ID) can open them. Locks made in Incognito are kept in memory only and are deleted when Chrome closes.
  • Touch ID, if you turn it on, creates a passkey saved by your operating system or password manager (such as iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager). Your fingerprint never leaves your device and GhostLock never sees it.
  • Your settings (lock style, cover and decoy sites, auto-lock and auto-close options, site lists) are saved in Chrome's local storage on this computer.
  • The last time each tab was used is kept in Chrome's session storage, only while Chrome is open. It contains internal tab numbers and times only.
  • Your Pro status and trial start date are cached on your device.

GhostLock Pro purchases

Pro is an optional subscription handled by ExtensionPay, with payments processed by Stripe. When you subscribe or restore a purchase, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe processes your payment details. GhostLock never sees or stores your card details. To check whether Pro is active, GhostLock asks ExtensionPay for your subscription status when you open the popup or settings, and a few times a day. The free trial runs on your device and needs no email.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share your data. The only third parties involved are ExtensionPay and Stripe, and only when you choose to subscribe.

Your choices

  • Uninstalling GhostLock removes its settings and stored data from your browser. Unlock your tabs first: locked pages can't be recovered after uninstalling.
  • You can delete GhostLock's Touch ID passkey from your password manager at any time.
  • To ask about or delete purchase records, contact us at the address below.
